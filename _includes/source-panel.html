{% comment %} Contextual source panel — slides in from the right (bottom sheet on mobile) when a citation marker is clicked, showing only the sources in that run. NON-MODAL BY DESIGN. Every reviewer's instinct will be "this should be role=dialog with a focus trap". It must not be. The whole point is that the reader keeps reading the article while the panel is open, so: ·
→ role="complementary" — a landmark, so screen-reader users have a jump route in, rather than a trap they have to escape. · no aria-modal — that tells AT everything outside is unavailable, the exact opposite of the requirement, and NVDA/JAWS switch to forms mode inside a dialog, which breaks browse-mode reading of the source text. · no focus trap, no inert, no scroll lock, no scrim, no outside-click close. It carries NO copy of the source text: on open it clones the
  • already rendered by sources.html, so the two can never drift and the page carries no duplicated JSON payload. See the SourcePanel IIFE in head.html. Hidden with visibility + an off-screen transform rather than the `hidden` attribute: `hidden` gives an entry-only animation via @starting-style and snaps shut, and this panel opens and closes dozens of times per reading session. {% endcomment %}
