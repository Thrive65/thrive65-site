The Coalition

## Who we are

Thrive65 is a community-based advocacy and engagement coalition dedicated to supporting a strong, equitable, and financially sustainable future for all students in District 65\. We bring together parents, caregivers, educators, community members, and stakeholders who believe that equitable, excellent schools require bold vision, expert implementation, and responsible stewardship.

## The current situation

* Enrollment down 25% since 2019   
* Two elementary schools already closed and buildings still likely to run under 70% utilized  
* $10 million in additional operating cuts required by FY30  
* 90 days cash on hand — bottom 5% of school districts statewide  
* A board split on whether to act now or gather more feedback first


## Our Pillars

### Equitable Excellence

Every student deserves access to an equitable, excellent education that addresses the unique needs of each individual student and the communities they represent. We believe that equity and excellence are not competing goals but mutually supportive commitments. By focusing on great teaching, strong programs, and the whole-child development of every student, we can make sure each of our schools is an environment where all children can thrive and reach their full potential.

### Financial Responsibility

To thrive, our district needs maintained facilities, a strategically aligned budget, and stable reserves. Without it, we can’t choose how to cultivate our kids, we just react to whatever crisis comes first. We need a budget that is balanced operationally and structurally- that means the increases in revenue or decreases in expenses to balance it are replicable each year. One-time influxes of revenue, refunds, or credits, are not acceptable in a truly balanced budget; those should be additive. A budget is not balanced if it reduces our annual commitment to building maintenance to avoid a loss.

Financial health isn’t at odds with our kids’ interests. It’s the foundation for them.

### Humanizing and Valuing Our Educators

Uncertainty has a cost, and our educators have carried it alongside us for years. Any real plan must treat the people doing the work — not just the buildings they work in — with clarity, respect, and fairness. Teachers, support staff, administrators, and school employees are the foundation of student success, and their expertise, dedication, and well-being must be valued and supported. D65 needs policies and practices that attract, develop, and retain exceptional, culturally responsive educators through respect, collaboration, and professional growth. When educators are empowered and supported, students benefit from stronger relationships, more engaging learning experiences, and better outcomes.
