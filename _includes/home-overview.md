## Who we are

Thrive65 is a community-based advocacy and engagement coalition dedicated to supporting a strong, equitable, and financially sustainable future for all students in District 65\. We bring together parents, caregivers, educators, community members, and stakeholders who believe that equitable, excellent schools require bold vision, expert implementation, and responsible stewardship.

## The facts we're organizing around

* Enrollment down 25% since 2019  
* Two elementary schools already closed and buildings still likely to run under 70% utilized  
* $10 million in additional operating cuts required by FY30  
* 90 days cash on hand — bottom 5% of school districts statewide  
* A board split on whether to act now or gather more feedback first
