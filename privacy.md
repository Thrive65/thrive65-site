Thrive65 is a community advocacy coalition. This page explains how we handle the information you share with us.

## What we collect

When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and nothing else.

## How we use it

We use your email address to send occasional updates about District 65 board meetings, budget decisions, and ways to get involved. We do not sell, trade, or share your email address with third parties.

## How it's stored

Email addresses are stored and managed through [EmailOctopus](https://emailoctopus.com), our email service provider. You can review their [privacy policy](https://emailoctopus.com/legal/privacy).

## Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can also contact us at [chetfarley@gmail.com](mailto:chetfarley@gmail.com) to be removed from the list.

## Questions

If you have questions about this policy, email [chetfarley@gmail.com](mailto:chetfarley@gmail.com).
